01 Front page

Whitespace declared "a luxury we can no longer afford"

In a stunning reversal, the Committee on Visual Restraint voted 12–0 to fill every pixel. Padding to be rationed. Margins available on the black market. Our correspondent reports from inside the grid.

02 Opinion

Your border-radius is a cry for help

Corners exist. Stop sanding them down. A 14-point argument, all of them sharp.

03 Typography

04 Markets

05 Weather

06 Investigation

Who killed the hamburger menu?

Everyone. It was everyone. A six-part exposé on navigation you can actually see, published all at once because pagination is a coward's game.

07 Classifieds

FOR SALE: gradient, barely used

Purple-to-teal, 45°, one careful owner (a fintech startup). Will trade for a single well-set paragraph. No reasonable offer refused.

We believe a website should look like a decision. Structure is the style. The grid is the message. If it doesn't work in black and white, it doesn't work — the yellow is a reward, not a rescue.

— The Editors, shouting from the composing room
