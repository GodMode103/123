一

The Waters

湯の効能

The spring rises at 68 degrees from a fault two kilometres beneath the inn, carrying sulphur, iron, and the patience of stone. Guests have soaked here through five eras; the water does not remember any of them, which is precisely its gift.

Rotenburo

The open-air bath faces the valley. In winter, steam and snowfall trade places in the air above the water.

42°C · outdoor

Hinoki Bath

Cypress planed by the fourth-generation carpenter. The scent deepens after ten minutes of stillness.

40°C · cedar hall

Private Spring

A stone basin for two, reserved by the hour. A single lantern. Nothing else is provided or needed.

二

Kaiseki

懐石料理
  • 先付 Sakizuke
    Mountain yam, ume blossom, first light
  • 椀物 Wanmono
    Clear broth of matsutake and yuzu rind
  • 向付 Mukōzuke
    River trout, cured three hours in kombu
  • 焼物 Yakimono
    Charcoal-grilled duck with sanshō
  • 水物 Mizumono
    Snow pear in chrysanthemum syrup

Dinner is served in your room at the hour you name. The menu is not chosen; it is what the mountain offered that morning. Our chef walks the ridge before dawn and the meal is his report.

三

Four Silences

四季折々
春

HARU

Cherry smoke drifts up the valley. The thaw makes the spring run one degree warmer.

夏

NATSU

Cicadas until dusk, then the river takes over. Evening baths under paper lanterns.

秋

AKI

The maples burn for three weeks. Kaiseki turns to chestnut, persimmon, first game.

冬

FUYU

Two metres of snow close the road. Guests who stay become, briefly, residents.
