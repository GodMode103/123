The Paper Theater

A Victorian toy theater built from CSS: a proscenium arch, striped curtains, and a stage whose scenery is four physical layers of "paper" arranged in real 3D space. One story, three acts, and the whole kingdom relights when the act changes.

Real depth, not fake depth

  • The stage is a perspective: 900px container; each scenery layer sits at an actual translateZ (10, 20, 45, 80px). Pointer movement rotates the parent scene a few degrees, and parallax emerges from geometry — no per-layer math, no JS positions. The browser's projection does the work.
  • Layers are hand-drawn SVG silhouettes (hills, pines, a cottage with two windows) stretched with preserveAspectRatio="none" and given paper thickness by a drop-shadow filter that reads as cut edges.

Relighting with custom properties

Every scene color — two sky stops, three paper layers, the moon, its glow, the cottage windows — is a CSS variable. The three act buttons swap a single class on <html>, and 1.2s transitions carry the whole set: morning gold → dusk violet → indigo night. The cottage windows go dark-to-lit because their fill is also a variable. Total relighting logic in JS: one line.

The writing

Three paragraphs, one per act, in storybook deadpan ("a sun that rose on schedule out of sheer politeness"). The final act breaks the fourth wall — "somewhere in the audience, a giant turned the page" — because a toy theater's audience is always a giant.

Iteration log — three passes

Pass 1 — Build

Proscenium, curtains, four Z-layers, tilt, act switcher, three story beats.

Pass 2 — Fine-tooth comb

Removed an invalid CSS declaration that had slipped into the header rules. Layers originally clipped at stage edges when tilted — extended them to −4% overdraw. Curtain stripes were flat: added inner shadowing and scalloped border-radius so they hang. Moon glow was on in daylight (wrong) — glow alpha is now itself a variable, zero in Act I.

Pass 3 — Complexify & polish

Marquee plate above the arch tracks the current act; buttons are proper aria-pressed toggles with focus rings; tilt disabled under reduced motion while act transitions collapse to instant cuts (a slide projector instead of a sunset — still legible). Windows given rounded corners so they read as paper cutouts, not pixels.

Files

One self-contained index.html. The scenery is ~40 SVG path points total.
