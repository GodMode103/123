ACT I — MORNING

In which the kingdom wakes

The kingdom was four hills, one cottage, and a sun that rose on schedule out of sheer politeness. Its entire population — whoever is holding this page — surveyed the realm each morning and found it adequate, which in small kingdoms is the highest possible praise.
