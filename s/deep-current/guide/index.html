Fable 25 · Guide 09

DEEPCURRENT — scroll as submersion

A scrollytelling documentary where the scrollbar is a depth winch: page position maps linearly onto a 10,935-meter descent. The design goal was for every system on the page — color, HUD, particles, copy — to answer to one variable: how deep are you?

One variable, four systems

  • Background: six color stops keyed to real ocean zones (epipelagic through Challenger Deep), piecewise-interpolated in RGB per scroll frame. The page darkens from rgb(4,92,132) to near-black rgb(0,3,7) exactly as the sea does — most of the darkening happens early, because that is physically true.
  • Depth HUD: a fixed instrument showing meters (interpolated between chapter depths, so the number always moves when you do), the named zone, and a glowing bead on a vertical gauge — your position in the water column.
  • Marine snow: a full-viewport canvas of ~90 slow particles with sinusoidal sway, falling perpetually — the real phenomenon that feeds the deep. Count scales with viewport width; the loop pauses in hidden tabs.
  • Parallax drifters: SVG creatures (a ray, current-lines) translating against scroll at 0.12–0.18× — enough to imply water without becoming a theme park.

The bioluminescence chapter

At −1,000 m the page grows 26 pulsing lights, each a span with randomized size, position, delay, and duration on one shared keyframe — so the field never visibly repeats. Their box-shadows do the glowing; there is no canvas involvement, and reduced-motion freezes them at mid-opacity where they read as a static starfield.

Editorial stance

Copy is written as a nature documentary in second person, and every chapter carries one verifiable fact styled as an instrument readout (.fact, monospace, keel-line left border). The facts are the emotional payload; the design's job is to get out of their way and keep darkening.

Iteration log — three passes

Pass 1 — Build

Depth mapping, color stops, HUD, five chapters + floor, marine snow, bio-dots, drifters.

Pass 2 — Fine-tooth comb

Zone label originally switched exactly at stop boundaries and flickered mid-chapter — now flips at the midpoint of each segment. Depth number jumped from 0 straight to 200 (hero wasn't a stop): added the surface stop at scroll 0. Drift parallax accumulated off-screen after long scrolls: wrapped with modulo. The resurface link respected smooth-scroll even under reduced motion — fixed to check the media query.

Pass 3 — Complexify & polish

Gauge bead position tied to total progress; marine-snow density made responsive (min(90, width/12)); visibility-change pause; sway added to snowfall so it drifts rather than rains; pressure/fact lines added per chapter; final CTA inverts to bio-green on hover. Contrast checked at every zone color against foam text.
