Fable 25 · Guide 05

Porcelain & Thumb — claymorphism with a spine

A ceramics studio rendered in the material it sells: every surface reads as soft-fired clay. The challenge with claymorphism is keeping it from turning to mush — this build pairs the softness with a strict two-shadow system and copy that stays dry.

The material system

  • Two shadows only: --pop (raised: warm shadow + white highlight) and --dent (pressed: the same pair, inset). Everything on the page is one or the other — cards pop, wells and steps dent. Consistent light source, top-left, always.
  • Squish physics: buttons scale down and translate on :active with a cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1) overshoot — the "clay gives, then springs back" feel. Hover lifts, press dents.
  • Morphing blobs: three background shapes animate border-radius through organic 4-corner values over 14s — clay being kneaded in slow motion.
  • Type pairing: Fraunces (an old-style serif with soft, thumbed terminals — the most "ceramic" typeface on Google Fonts) against round, friendly Nunito.

The glaze picker

The vase is an inline SVG filled with a gradient whose stops reference var(--glaze) and var(--glaze-hi). The five swatch buttons just swap two CSS custom properties on :root — no SVG manipulation, no repaint logic, five glazes for ~10 lines of JS. Swatches are real buttons with aria-pressed state and a visible ✓ on selection.

Iteration log — three passes

Pass 1 — Build

Hero with plinth + vase, glaze system, collection cards, four-step process, workshop band, blobs.

Pass 2 — Fine-tooth comb

Shadow values were muddy grey originally — recast both shadow colors from the clay hue (warm brown at low alpha) so depth reads as material, not smoke. Blobs sat above content and intercepted clicks: pinned to z-index:0 + pointer-events:none. The charred glaze failed contrast for its ✓ mark — added text-shadow. Fixed blob overflow causing horizontal scroll at 360px.

Pass 3 — Complexify & polish

Added the highlight stroke on the vase (a white brush glint), drop-shadow under it tied to the glaze depth, stepped counters in the process cards (counter(step)), keyboard focus rings on every interactive element, and reduced-motion kills blob morphing and squish transitions. Copy pass: every price tag and card line rewritten until it sounded like a potter, not a template.
