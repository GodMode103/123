Fable 25 · Guide 03

NIGHTSHIFT.LOG — a romance in green phosphor

The bet: a website that is a single physical object — a 1983 CRT terminal — and a story you excavate rather than scroll. The design work is split evenly between simulating the hardware and writing four letters good enough to justify the frame.

CRT physics, all in CSS

  • Scanlines: a 3px repeating-linear-gradient — dark 1px line, 2px gap.
  • Aperture grille: a second repeating gradient of 1px red/green/blue tints at 2.5% opacity, perpendicular to the scanlines — the shadow-mask RGB structure of a real tube.
  • Phosphor glow: double text-shadow (tight 6px halo + wide 22px bloom).
  • Tube curvature: an elliptical radial-gradient vignette plus a diagonal glass-glare streak on one overlay.
  • Flicker: a 4.1s keyframe animation that dips opacity for ~50ms twice per cycle — enough to feel analog, rare enough not to annoy. Disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • The bezel: the monitor shell is a gradient, border-radius and an engraved model number — the page has a physical prop, not a background.

The typing engine

  • Variable cadence: 14–40ms per character, ~90ms after commas, ~170ms after sentence-enders — mimicking a human transcription rather than a metronome.
  • Skippable: any keypress or tap flushes the current letter instantly. Impatience is a valid reading speed.
  • Accessible: the screen is aria-live="polite"; menu entries are real <button>s reachable by keyboard (keys 1–4 also work); reduced-motion renders all text instantly.

The writing

Four letters, ~120 words each, structured as a complete arc: notice → flirtation → risk → answer. The last letter is flagged never sent, and the archive footnote (a 1983 change-of-address form) resolves the story in bureaucratic deadpan. Copy is doing half the design here on purpose.

Iteration log — three passes

PASS 1 — build

Bezel/tube/screen stack, overlays, typing engine, boot sequence, four letters, menu system.

PASS 2 — fine-tooth comb

Letters overflowed the tube on phones — line lengths reworked to ~58 chars and font clamped. The flicker fired every 1.5s originally: nauseating; retimed to twice per 4.1s. Menu rows were plain text; replaced with real buttons (hover highlight, focus rule, keyboard). Fixed double-open race when mashing number keys (busy flag).

PASS 3 — complexify & polish

Added the aperture-grille RGB layer and glass glare; punctuation-aware typing cadence; skip-on-any-input; auto-scroll pinned to the newest line; the DRAFTS letter and archive footnote added to give the story an ending worth the visit.

Files

One self-contained index.html. One font (VT323). Zero images — the monitor is drawn entirely in CSS.
