Fable 25 · Guide 02

HELIOSPHERE — a star computed live

The design bet: instead of a NASA photo, render the Sun itself — procedurally, per-frame, in the visitor's GPU — and build a research-station interface around it. No three.js, no shader library: one hand-written WebGL2 fragment shader (~90 lines of GLSL).

How the sun works

  • Sphere from a disk: for every pixel inside the solar radius, recover the sphere surface point via z = √(R² − r²) — a full raymarcher isn't needed for a single sphere.
  • Convection cells: 5-octave 3D value-noise FBM, domain-warped (feeding one FBM into the coordinates of the next) and advected over time on three different speed layers — this is what makes the surface churn rather than slide.
  • Limb darkening: brightness scaled by pow(z/R, 0.55), the real optical phenomenon that makes a star's edge dimmer than its center.
  • Corona: exponential falloff from the limb whose decay rate is itself modulated by angular FBM — producing streamers instead of a uniform glow.
  • Temperature color ramp: ember → plasma → white-hot mapped through two smoothsteps, so bright regions read as hotter, not just lighter.
  • Parallax: the pointer eases into a uniform (`M`) that offsets the view — subtle, spring-damped in JS, never jarring.

Type & palette

Space Grotesk for display/body, IBM Plex Mono for telemetry. Palette derived from black-body radiation: near-black void #050208, ember #c2451e, plasma #ffb347, white-hot #fff3d6. The gradient headline uses the same ramp as the shader — UI and simulation share one physical logic.

Iteration log — three passes

Pass 1 — Build

Shader (noise, FBM, limb darkening, corona), hero HUD, instruments grid, telemetry counters, chronicle table.

Pass 2 — Fine-tooth comb

Corona originally overwhelmed the headline at narrow viewports — dropped the sun 12% and added a vignette. Granulation looked like sliding texture, not convection; fixed by domain-warping with a second time-advected FBM. Starfield was visible through the corona (wrong); now masked by radius. Added a pure-CSS radial-gradient fallback when WebGL2 is unavailable, and shader-compile failure now restores that fallback instead of a black void.

Pass 3 — Complexify & polish

Dark filament arcs added (second threshold FBM) for realism at the limbs. DPR capped at 1.75 to keep full-screen fragment work fast on 4K displays. prefers-reduced-motion renders exactly one frame — a still portrait of the star. Tab-hidden pauses the RAF loop. Counter animation eases with cubic-out and honors reduced motion by printing final values instantly.
