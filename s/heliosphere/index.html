01 / Instruments

Three ways of staring at something you cannot look at

Each instrument isolates one voice from the star's noise. Together they make the only weather report that matters to every living thing at once.

INST-α

Coronagraph Array

An artificial eclipse, held forever. The occulting disk hides the photosphere so the million-degree corona can be seen breathing.

INST-β

Magnetograph

Maps the field lines that knot, snap, and reconnect — the trigger mechanism of every flare we have ever recorded.

INST-γ

Helioseismometer

The Sun rings like a bell struck five minutes ago, always. We read its interior by listening to the surface tremble.
